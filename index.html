The
Open-Graded
Method

The open-graded method uses clean, crushed stone to create a strong, stable patio base that allows water to drain naturally.

Unlike traditional methods, which use compacted gravel topped with bedding sand, it helps prevent water buildup, shifting, and settling over time.

Drains naturally

Water moves down through the stone instead of building up under your patio.

Stays put

Less trapped water means less shifting when the ground freezes and thaws.

Won't settle

A strong, stable stone base that holds its shape over time.

Tom Nobles

Founder, Marathon Outdoors

Tom Nobles grew up in Cary, North Carolina, and developed an interest in landscaping through mowing lawns and taking on landscaping projects while in college.

He went on to study mechanical engineering at UNC Charlotte, where he competed in Division I cross country and track. His passion for running continued beyond college, eventually earning him a qualifying time for the 2020 U.S. Olympic Marathon Trials.

After working in engineering, Tom decided to turn his entrepreneurial interests and passion for outdoor design into a business. In 2022, he founded Marathon Outdoors, combining his technical background, hands-on experience, and attention to detail to create beautiful, functional outdoor spaces.
